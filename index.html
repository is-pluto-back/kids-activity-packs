Review Exams

For Mom & Dad

Parent PIN. See every exam with date, score and grade, add notes, then release results to the kids.

How it works

  1. Open a pack, choose subjects, the number of questions and an optional timer.
  2. Press Start Exam. Questions are mixed at random from the question bank. No answers or hints are shown during the exam.
  3. Press Submit Exam - one submission per exam. It's saved online with the date and time.
  4. Mom opens Review Exams on any phone or computer (parent PIN), checks the answers, adds notes and presses Release.
  5. The kids see their right/wrong answers, Mom's notes and their grade under My Exams.
